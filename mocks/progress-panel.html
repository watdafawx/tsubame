Operations panel (mock, B11-E)

Flat, no shadows, tokens from tokens.json (one new: ok green). Pick a state, toggle theme and scale. Everything is clickable: Pause / Resume, Cancel, expand, collapse to the status-bar pill, Undo, Show details, Retry, Skip and the conflict buttons. Auto-dismiss of finished cards is 5 s (hover holds it). The simulation here ticks at 10 Hz and stops when nothing runs, as the real one must.

Panel

Docked bottom-right over the list, 344 px wide, one card per operation, newest finished on top of the running ones. Opens by itself 400 ms after an operation starts (quick ones never flash; the status bar text stays as it is today). Collapse chevron folds it to the pill, the pill reopens it.

Cards

Verb + percent, "N items from A to B" (folders as names, full path in a tooltip), 4 px bar, bytes, speed + remaining, 10 s speed sparkline. Delete shows items instead of bytes. Pause / Resume, Cancel, expand for the files being processed.

Queue

The queue is FIFO with one worker, so a second operation shows "Waiting" with an empty bar and only Cancel. It starts by itself when the one before ends.

Finished

One line, green check, "Done, Undo", 2 px countdown line, gone after 5 s (hover holds). Errors never auto-dismiss: "Show details", Retry failed, Skip. Cancelled shows "Cancelled after 6 of 14" with Undo.

Conflict

App-modal dialog in the window (no scrim: no alpha). The newer file gets the accent border and a "Newer" chip. Suggested button is filled: Replace when incoming is newer, Skip otherwise. The old file goes to the Recycle Bin. Keys: R, S, K, Esc.

Cost

No timer while nothing runs. Progress repaints at most 10 Hz, only the changed card (bar, numbers, sparkline). One one-shot timer for the earliest finished-card dismiss. Idle = 0 wake-ups.
