Inline rename

Task U3 mock (2 of 2). F2 turns the name cell of the focused row into a text field in place: no dialog, no layout shift (row height stays). The kit already has the text input (caret, selection, clipboard, IME); this is that widget inside a cell. Drawing needs only a filled rect, a 1 px stroke, a 2 px line and text.

Theme Density

1. F2 on a file

The name cell becomes a field fill with a 1 px focus border. The stem (everything before the last dot) is pre-selected with a selected rect, the extension stays unselected, a solid caret sits at the end of the selection (no blink timer beyond the kit’s 10 s focus caret). Enter and Esc caps sit at the right end of the field; the status bar repeats them. Click elsewhere commits; Esc cancels. Dotfiles and folders select the whole name. Rename goes through the C3 queue, so Ctrl+Z undoes it. Other columns keep their text; the row keeps its selection fill.

2. Field states

Validation runs on every keystroke. Invalid: border and a 2 px underline in danger, plus a one-line reason bubble (popup fill, 1 px danger border) under the field. Enter is ignored while invalid, the field never closes by itself. Other reasons use the same bubble: “Name cannot be empty”, “Name cannot end with a space or period”, ““CON” is a reserved name”, “Path is too long”. Conflict is checked against the folder listing (case-insensitive, as NTFS).

3. Batch rename: entry bar and live preview

F2 with two or more rows selected opens a docked bar under the toolbar (list below stays visible and selected): title with the count, a pattern field (tokens in accent), Apply (accent fill) and Cancel. The preview is a popup under the bar listing old → new for every row (first 8, then “+ N more”), updating on every keystroke. Extension is kept automatically. Apply is one undo step. Tokens in this mock: {name} (stem), {n} (1, 2, 3…), {date}; the full rule set (find/replace, case, regex) belongs to F4 and reuses this bar.

4. Batch rename: collisions

Pattern Invoice (no {n}) makes names collide. Colliding rows get a selected_inactive row fill, a danger new name and a “duplicate” flag; the header counts them, the footer says how to fix it, and Apply is disabled (outlined, text_disabled). All-or-nothing: nothing is renamed until every name is unique and none exists in the folder.
